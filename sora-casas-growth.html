04 · THE CONTENT ENGINE

How content becomes rankings and traffic.

PILLARS · 12

Authority pages

2,500–4,500 words on one big, high-intent topic: “cost to build a house in Panama,” “Friendly Nations Visa real estate,” “Boquete vs Pedasí.” Each pillar targets one big keyword.

1 pillar → 1 big keyword + 10–20 long-tail
CLUSTERS · 60

Supporting posts

1,000–2,000 words answering specific questions that link to the pillar: “can you get title in Bocas?”, “septic cost in Pedasí.” Each cluster captures long-tail and strengthens the pillar.

5 clusters per pillar · all link up
