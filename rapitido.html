MISSION

A delivery app where everyone wins — the restaurant keeps more margin, the driver earns a salary, the customer pays less and waits less. The economics work, the tech works. What was missing was the decision.

RAPITIDO doesn't compete with PedidosYa, Uber Eats, and DiDi Food on restaurant count — it competes on structure. 18% commission (vs PedidosYa's 28%). Salaried-plus-tips drivers (no piecework). Live stock from the POS. Native dietary filters. Pay-in-HILO discount. And two products in one: the delivery app + a Restaurant OS (table, reservations, inventory). We start in Boquete and David, where La Compañía lives. Then Panama City, then El Salvador.

What each side wins.

More margin, more data

  • Restaurant OS included — reservations + table + inventory on one platform.
  • 7-day payout (vs PedidosYa's 14-30)
SIDE 02 · DRIVER

Base salary + tips

  • 100% of tips — Rapitido takes nothing.
  • Health insurance from month 6. Vehicle insurance included.
  • Multi-stop bonus — picking up at 2 restaurants in one trip = +$2 per order.
  • Electric scooter/bike financing · $0 down · salary deduction
  • Access to La Compañía benefits (Sora · medicine · Plata)
SIDE 03 · CUSTOMER

Faster, cheaper

  • $9.99/mo subscription = unlimited free delivery. Cheaper than 3 PedidosYa orders.
  • Live stock — if the kitchen runs out of ceviche, the item grays instantly. No 20-min wait for the call.
  • Multi-stop — sushi + dessert from a different restaurant in one order.
  • Real dietary filters — vegan, gluten-free, halal, keto, anti-inflammatory. Optional Sora-Medicines link.
  • Pay in HILO = 2% discount. Crypto-native, optional, frictionless for non-users.
  • WhatsApp ordering for chat lovers — conversational bot with full menu.

03 · FEATURES

The differences that matter.

Not a checklist. These actually change the experience.

01 · MULTI-STOP
Two restaurants, one driver

Order sushi from one + dessert from another. Driver hits both in optimal order. Cheaper than two deliveries, faster in dense zones.

02 · LIVE STOCK
What you see, you get

Direct POS integration with the restaurant. When the chef sells the last ceviche, the item grays in milliseconds. Zero 'we don't have that' calls.

03 · NATIVE DIET
Filters that actually filter

Vegan, gluten-free, halal, keto, anti-inflammatory. Restaurants tag dish by dish. If you're on metformin or GLP-1, the Sora-Medicines filter suggests what's compatible.

04 · WHATSAPP BOT

Many in LATAM don't want another app. RAPITIDO has an official WhatsApp bot: 'hi, I want ceviche' → menu → confirm → ETA → pay. No download, no login.

05 · HILO PAYMENT

04 · RESTAURANT OS

Two products, one company.

Delivery is only half. The other half is the OS the restaurant uses every day.

PedidosYa is a delivery platform. RAPITIDO is that plus an OS for restaurants. When a restaurant joins, they get: point of sale (POS), table management with QR-menu, online reservations, real-time inventory, analytics dashboard, and the delivery-app integration. All unified.

That changes the economics. PedidosYa only earns when someone orders delivery. RAPITIDO earns from four streams: delivery commission + monthly POS SaaS + reservation commission + payment-processing spread. Each stream is small, but together they make a healthier business than living off 28% commissions.

Why the restaurant adopts

Today a restaurant owner in Panama pays: Vendus (POS) $30/mo + OpenTable (reservations) $60/mo + 28% PedidosYa commission. RAPITIDO offers all that for $45/mo + 18% commission. And the three systems talk to each other.

STACK · RESTAURANT OS

Everything the restaurant gets

  • POS — point of sale for waiters, integrated with kitchen (KDS) and menu. Works offline.
  • QR-MENU — customer scans, orders from their phone, pays directly. Cuts waiter error by 90%.
  • RESERVATIONS — reservation engine with waitlist + SMS/WhatsApp reminders. Live table mapping.
  • INVENTORY — stock auto-decrements as it sells. Re-order alerts. Auto-costing.
  • DELIVERY — app orders flow into the same POS. Kitchen doesn't use a second device.
  • ANALYTICS — top dishes, margin per dish, peak hour, food cost trend. Data PedidosYa never shared.
  • PAYMENTS — card, cash, HILO. One terminal. 7-day settlement.
