09 · QUESTIONS

The essentials.

What does “verified” mean?

We check identity, trade, and references before a pro is listed. The green check isn't for sale — it's earned, and it can be lost.

What does it cost the client?

Nothing. MANO is free for people seeking a service. Pros pay for qualified leads or a verification + featured membership.

How do I know the price is fair?

Every profile shows the pro's rate against the market average for their category and zone, with the min–max range. No surprise bills.

How is it different from a Facebook group?

Verification, reviews tied to real jobs, transparent pricing, and a match that tells you why. A network that improves with every job, not a feed that scrolls away.
