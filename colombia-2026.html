The one-paragraph thesis
Colombia just elected its most pro-business, pro-US president in a generation, the peso is at a 6-year high, and FDI is returning, exactly as you'd be entering. The right move is not to pick one business; it's to build a curated community/events engine first (your instinct, and the correct one), use it to meet the right people and earn trust, and let that network throw off the higher-margin ventures you already have pieces of: Tez (beauty, in the world's beauty capital), medical-tourism concierge, AI-nearshore services, and eventually real estate. The single biggest risk is reputational and now literally at the border: Medellín is expelling the "foreign-men-party" economy. Build the classy version and the crackdown becomes your moat.

01The political inflection: "El Tigre" won, and it's a tailwind

"El Tigre" = Abelardo de la Espriella. He won the June 21, 2026 runoff against the leftist Iván Cepeda by the narrowest margin in Colombian history (~49.66% vs 48.70%, ~250k votes), inaugurated August 7, 2026. He self-branded as "El Tigre" (supporters = "la manada"); Trump congratulated "'El Tigre' (THE TIGER!)" by name. A Miami-and-Bogotá criminal-defense lawyer and political newcomer, he ran independent on "Defensores de la Patria," backed by the Uribista base and evangelicals. His VP is José Manuel Restrepo, a former finance minister markets trust, which is why the agenda reads as credible.

Why it's a tailwind for a US, crypto-wealthy founder

  • Direction of travel: lower taxes (promises 35%→30% corporate, abolish the wealth tax, kill the 4×1,000), deregulation, a pivot back to Washington and away from China, hardline security (ending Petro's failed "paz total"), 6–7% growth ambition. The win triggered a peso rally to ~3,425/USD, strongest in ~6 years; analysts expect "the return of capital."
  • Underwrite on today's rules, not the promises. He has no congressional majority and inherits a fiscal crisis, so cuts will be slow. In force now: 35% corporate tax, 20% non-resident dividend withholding (no US treaty relief), a wealth tax, and new DIAN crypto-reporting (Res. 000240, transactions >$50k auto-flagged, first big report May 2027).

Bottom line

About as favorable a macro-political inflection as Colombia offers. Treat the de la Espriella agenda as upside option value, not your base case.

02The strategy: community first, then the flywheel

Your instinct, events as the immediate play that develops into a larger flywheel, is right, and the evidence backs a specific version. What works (asset-light, recurring): Hampton ($8.5k/yr founder community, ~$8M ARR, events just break even), EO/YPO, South Park Commons (community → a $500M fund), Summit → Powder Mountain (events → real estate). What fails: capital-heavy physical clubs with big leases (Soho House's chronic losses, The Wing, NeueHouse bankruptcy).

Events should break even. The community is the asset. Spin-offs are where the money is. Stay asset-light until you have operating partners.

05Tez — the crown-jewel intersection

Medellín is a global beauty capital, a medical-tourism magnet, and an influencer-economy city. Tez sits at the center of all three. The upgrade for a resident founder: in tez.html, Tez is a Panama Free-Zone duty-free re-export play with Colombia as the top export market. Keep that supply backbone, but add a Medellín-native brand layer that captures the beauty-capital halo the re-export framing leaves on the table.

Four channels (in order of trust)

  1. Clinics/spas/salons (B2B): post-procedure skincare (SPF, healing serums) into the exact clinics doing the surgeries; recurring buyers who prescribe to patients.
  2. Content/influencer DTC (your edge): "the skincare of the world's beauty capital"; recruit Colombia's ~2–2.5M catalog-reseller army.
  3. Retail/pharmacy: needs INVIMA NSO (valid 7 yrs, Andean bloc; Colombian NIT + RETHUS químico farmacéutico).
  4. Medical-tourist take-home: patients flying home as a distribution channel ("I got my glow in Medellín" kit).

Anchor product: SPF

Colombia sunscreen demand grew ~63% (2022→2027 est.) — tropical, daily, high-repurchase, and the natural post-procedure product. Lead with SPF + healing serums (matches the "lead with hard data" finding in tez.html). Tez is the highest-margin, most-defensible asset, but a 12–24 month brand build; fund it with the faster cashflow plays while it matures.

06The honest dark side (read twice)

The border crackdown targets exactly the wrong events business

Mayor Fico Gutiérrez is running a popular war on sex tourism: prostitution bans in El Poblado/Provenza, minor curfews, hotel closures, and crucially 600+ foreigners denied entry at Medellín's airport in early 2026 (vs 110 in all of 2025), mostly US citizens, some with 10-year bans, flagged via luggage/phone searches and the US "Angel Watch" data-share. They target "passport bros" running "parties for foreigners." An events brand marketing parties for foreign men builds the exact profile that gets people banned. Non-negotiable: mixed-gender, founder/wellness/dining framing, vetted guest lists, locals visibly included, zero "parties for foreigners" marketing. The crackdown is killing your sleazy competition — it's a moat for a clean operator.

  • Scopolamine ("Devil's Breath") drink-spiking via dating apps, concentrated in Parque Lleras; a cluster of suspicious US-citizen deaths. Keep events out of that exposure.
  • Extortion ("vacuna") ~$1B/yr; ~380 combos, "todos pagan." Another reason to avoid cash-nightlife/physical-party venues (highest extortion + AML/narco-front surface).
  • Gentrification backlash ("digital nomads, temporary colonizers"). Mitigate by employing/including locals and avoiding the displacing STR model.
  • Level 3 travel advisory; Medellín city homicide at record lows (~11/100k) but rural Antioquia deteriorating (Gulf Clan).

The clean-operator test

Pick businesses that (a) employ locals, (b) earn in USD or build a real brand/asset, (c) have no nightlife/sex/drug adjacency, (d) are fully formalized. Beauty, medical-tourism concierge, AI-services, 30+ day housing, and a curated community all pass. Nightlife and party-tourism fail.

07Legal / tax / visa setup

  • Entity: SAS — 100% foreign ownership, no minimum capital, ~3–6 weeks; formed remotely via apostilled POA; a local legal representative is required; register foreign capital with Banco de la República (Form 4) — this is what later lets you repatriate profits.
  • Visa: M (Migrant) investor, not the Digital Nomad Visa (now ~42% rejection rate, no local income, doesn't count toward residency). Own-company route ~100 SMMLV (~$46k) with work permit; property route 350 SMMLV (~$150–165k), no work permit. 5 continuous years on M → R (permanent).
  • Tax traps: >183 days in any 365-day window = tax resident, taxed on worldwide income (relevant to crypto). No US–Colombia treaty (relief only via US FTC/FEIE). DIAN crypto reporting from 2026. Don't over-stay before you invest — crossing 183 days first can disqualify your money from FDI registration and block the investor visa.
  • In force: IVA 19%, corporate 35% (small co. can opt into Régimen Simple), ICA, 4×1,000, mandatory e-invoicing, monthly contador.
  • Sector: Tez retail needs INVIMA NSO; 30+ day rentals need RNT registration; events need alcaldía permits (Ley 1493) + OSA (Sayco-Acinpro) music licensing.

08The sequenced roadmap

Phase 0 — Legal + recon (weeks 0–4)

Immigration lawyer; set up the SAS with a vetted local legal rep; choose the M-investor path; plan the 183-day line. Join MDE Community, DareToShift, AmCham, run/padel/wellness communities. Stand up Luma + content.

Phase 1 — Cashflow + network (months 1–3)

Launch the events portfolio (free wellness morning + supper club + sponsor mixer). In parallel start the content studio, take first medical-tourism concierge referrals and first AI-nearshore US clients (the fast-cash plays). Convert the best people into a paid founding membership (~day 60–90).

Phase 2 — Brand build (months 3–12)

Fund Tez with Phase-1 cashflow: INVIMA registration, clinic relationships, content-led DTC, SPF-first launch. Formalize referral-fee deals (real estate, clinics, wellness, relocation) off the membership base.

Phase 3 — Real assets (year 2+)

Deploy capital into 30+ day recovery-stay housing that feeds the medical-tourism funnel; real-estate co-investment only with operating partners (the Summit/Powder Mountain lesson: amateurs lose money on the real-estate spin-off). A physical Tez clinic-spa is the last thing, once the brand justifies it.

Content + community generate the network and the fast cash; the network de-risks Tez and the concierge; the concierge fills the housing; the housing sells Tez aftercare. Each layer funds and feeds the next, and none of it touches the part of the market that gets foreigners banned.
