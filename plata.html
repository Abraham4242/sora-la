MISSION

Moving money across the Americas should cost cents, arrive in seconds, and reveal only what you choose. Today it doesn't. We're fixing it.

PLATA is a P2P payment network, native onchain. Dollar-pegged stablecoins (USDC + USDT) running on crypto rails, accessed through an app that feels like Venmo but with selective privacy, multi-layer biometric auth, and seconds-to-settle. It starts in the most natural corridor — Panama and El Salvador, two dollarized countries with cross-diaspora populations. From there, all of LATAM.

02 · THE CORRIDOR

Panama ↔ El Salvador. Why this axis.

Not generic LATAM. Two USD-twin countries — both with crypto appetite.

Both economies share something unique in the region: USD as the real currency. No FX rates, no FX commissions, no bank spreads. A USDC sent in Boquete is worth exactly a USDC received in San Salvador.

El Salvador legalized Bitcoin as currency in 2021. Approved the digital-asset issuer framework in 2023. Has the most crypto-friendly regulation in the Americas. Panama is a regional banking hub with active EMI licenses and a diaspora-remitter community to Central America.

What the corridor looks like today

There are ~300K Salvadorans in Panama sending remittances. ~80K Panamanians working/studying in Central America. Current corridor volume: ~$1.2B annually via bank wires + MoneyGram/Western Union, with average commissions of 4-7% per transfer. PLATA charges ~0.3% and settles in seconds.

04 · TECH STACK

What's under the hood.

Four layers. Each chosen for a specific reason.

RAILS · ONCHAIN
Solana + Tron

Solana for speed and low fees (400ms finality, $0.001/tx). Tron for stablecoin volume (TRC-20 USDT moves $50B/day). Native bridge between both.

USDC · USDT · USD · TRX · SOL
PRIVACY · ZK
zk-SNARKs · shielded transfers

Zero-knowledge proofs to hide amounts and counterparties — optional, not required. For compliance (KYC/AML): selective proofs signed by the user only when off-ramping to fiat.

RISC ZERO · ZKsync stack · CIRCOM
AUTH · BIOMETRIC
FIDO2 + adaptive layers

FIDO2 passkeys (W3C standard). For <$100: Face ID or fingerprint. For $100-1000: + voice phrase. For >$1000: + second factor on separate device. Friction level scales with amount.

Local banks at each end

Agreements with Banco General + Multibank in PA and Banco Cuscatlán + Banco Agrícola in SV. Local ACH each country. Cash-in / cash-out via retail agents (Strike/Chivo-style).

05 · PRIVACY

Selective privacy. Not privacy-or-compliance.

The default is private. Compliance is opt-in when needed.

Venmo has a public feed where your neighbor sees you paid your therapist. Yappy reports every transaction to Panama's central bank. Wise reports to Treasury. PLATA defaults to amount + counterparty privacy, while preserving the ability to generate cryptographic compliance proofs when you need them (off-ramp, tax filing, etc).

PLATA holds EMI licenses (Electronic Money Institution) in both PA and SV. The regulator always holds an audit key — but only under judicial order, not by default.

SCENARIOS · WHO SEES WHAT

Three modes per transaction

  • PRIVATE (default) — only sender + receiver see amount + memo. Onchain only shows an encrypted commitment.
  • MERCHANT — for store payments. Merchant receives identity + amount. Allows invoice issuance. Customer can hide counterparty if they want.
  • COMPLIANCE — the user signs a proof saying 'this transfer was legal, complies with X thresholds'. The authority reads only what's necessary, not the whole account.
  • Mode is chosen per transaction — not per account. The app asks before signing.
  • The user holds the view keys. Without them, nobody — not even La Compañía — can read details.
