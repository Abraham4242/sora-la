# SORA · Costa Rica Chapter

*Tier 1 · Priority launch · Target go-live: Q2 2027*

## Thesis

Costa Rica is the most established premium-travel market in Central America. "Pura Vida" sells itself. Large US expat community in Nosara, Tamarindo, and Manuel Antonio. The market is saturated with mid-tier yoga retreats — Sora's win is positioning further up: micro-cohort (8-12 guests max), name chefs, true exclusivity. Guanacaste real estate is hot and the kind of guest who pays for our weekends is the same guest looking for a second home.

Most expensive setup of Tier 1 but highest LTV per customer.

## Legal entity + ops

**Vehicle:** Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada (SRL). Cost: $2,500 + minimum capital. ~30 days.

**Firms:** Outlier Legal Services (expat-focused, English) · Quatro Legal · BLP Legal

**Bank:** BAC Credomatic or BCR (state bank, slower but solid). USDC ramp: most brokers accept USDC OTC; for retail use Crypto.com or Binance via personal off-ramp.

**Hosts:** 2 — one Nosara, one Manuel Antonio. $2,000-2,500/mo each. Recruit through Bodhi Tree alumni network, Selina/Habitas circuit.

**Talent:** Maxi Jiménez (Apotecario chef), local yoga teachers (Sara Tomas Levy at Bodhi Tree), surf instructors (Coconut Harry's), wellness practitioners.

## Risks

- **Saturated mid-tier** — must stay differentiated up-market
- **High lodging costs** — Lapa Rios is $1,200/night/room; margin pressure
- **Rainy season** (May-November) on Pacific — skip Sora events July-October
- **Real estate title issues** — Costa Rica land titles can be murky; mandatory title insurance
