## Legal entity setup

**Vehicle:** Sociedad Anónima Promotora de Inversión (SAPI) or Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada (SRL).
- SAPI is more flexible for foreign investors and easier for outside capital later.
- Cost: $3-5k legal fees, ~30 days to form.

**Recommended firms:**
- **Mijares, Angoitia, Cortés y Fuentes** (top-tier, Mexico City) — for SAPI + ongoing counsel
- **Galicia Abogados** (mid-tier, faster) — for straightforward SRL setup
- **De la Vega & Conde** (boutique, real estate specialty) — for the real estate arm

**Critical:** foreign ownership in the "restricted zone" (50km from coast, 100km from borders) requires either a fideicomiso (bank trust) or operating through a Mexican corporation. For Tulum/Sayulita/Valle de Guadalupe properties, the corporate-ownership route is the answer.

**Bank:** BBVA México or Santander México for corporate account. USDC ramp via Bitso (Mexican exchange, regulated, integrates with banks).

## Specific risks

- **Cartel-related insurance optics** in certain Pacific routes (avoid Guerrero state for weekends, even though Puerto Escondido is in Oaxaca). Stick to Oaxaca coast (Mazunte, Zipolite), not the Guerrero side.
- **Tulum saturation** — be visibly different from the EDM-festival crowd. Position Sora as "post-Tulum" — Sian Ka'an, not Papaya Playa.
- **Mexican peso volatility** — price in USD, but invoice in pesos for tax compliance. Use a 5% buffer on conversion.
- **Restricted zone real estate** — must use corporate ownership or fideicomiso. Do not let a guest do an individual purchase in restricted zone.
