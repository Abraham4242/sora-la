# SORA · Argentina Chapter

*Tier 1 · Priority launch · Target go-live: Q1 2027*

## Thesis

Argentina has the most European-sensibility design culture in LATAM and the most favorable currency arbitrage for USD-holding guests. A 5-star wine-country weekend in Mendoza costs what a 3-star Tulum weekend costs. Buenos Aires is the natural design hub for the entire Southern Cone. Patagonia (Bariloche, El Chaltén) and Salta (north) round out a country that has three distinct premium products: city + wine + wilderness.

Argentina + Uruguay can be run as one operational unit (shared host, shared lawyer) — see also [UY-uruguay.md](UY-uruguay.md).

## Real estate strategy

Argentine real estate is the most controversial in LATAM — capital controls, inflation, BUT massive USD-cash discounts. Right now (2026) is a buyer's market.

### Buenos Aires · Palermo / Recoleta / Puerto Madero
- 1BR Palermo apartment: $90k-$180k (USD cash purchase, "blue dollar" advantageous)
- 2BR Recoleta classic: $200k-$500k

## Legal entity setup

**Vehicle:** Sociedad Anónima Simplificada (SAS) — 2017 reform made this fast and foreigner-friendly.
- 1 shareholder minimum, low capital ($300 USD equivalent)
- Cost: $2-3k legal, ~30 days

**Recommended firms:**
- **Marval O'Farrell Mairal** (top-tier Buenos Aires, English-speaking)
- **Bruchou & Funes de Rioja** (corporate)
- **Estudio Beccar Varela** (boutique, good for SMEs and real estate)

**Critical:** every foreign shareholder needs a CDI (similar to CPF in Brazil — tax ID for non-residents). Get this first via consulate.

**Bank:** Banco Galicia or Santander Argentina for corporate. The "blue dollar" market means most transactions still happen in USD cash outside the formal banking system — operate accordingly with a real accountant. **USDC + Lemon Cash or Ripio for crypto ramp.**

## Specific risks

- **Inflation** — domestic supplier prices reset constantly. Lock in USD-denominated contracts where possible.
- **Capital controls** — getting money out of Argentina is hard. Solution: receive payments in USDC abroad, only convert what's needed for ops.
- **Peronist policy volatility** — every administration changes the rules. Milei era is favorable to USD-business; subsequent administrations may not be.
- **Patagonia weather** — October-March is the only viable Patagonia season for premium guests. Cluster events accordingly.
