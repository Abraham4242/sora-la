# SORA · Dominican Republic Chapter

*Tier 3 · Target go-live: Q3 2028*

## Thesis

The Dominican Republic has the most polished Caribbean tourism infrastructure in LATAM and the largest European expat community in the region. Las Terrenas (Samaná) is the design-led alternative to Punta Cana (which is the all-inclusive market we avoid). Santo Domingo's Zona Colonial is the oldest European city in the Americas and a real architectural product. The DR's strategic advantage: easy flights from both Europe and the US, no visa friction for most premium guests, USD widely accepted alongside the peso.

## Real estate strategy

DR real estate is the most foreigner-friendly in the Caribbean. No restrictions on foreign ownership. USD-denominated transactions standard.

- Las Terrenas oceanfront condos: $200k-$800k
- Cap Cana villas: $500k-$5M
