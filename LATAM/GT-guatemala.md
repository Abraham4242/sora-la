# SORA · Guatemala Chapter

*Tier 3 · Target go-live: Q4 2028*

## Thesis

Guatemala is the most photographically rich country in Central America. Antigua's cobblestone streets, Lake Atitlán's three volcanoes, Tikal's pre-classic Maya pyramids — every weekend produces hero photography. Smaller premium market than the rest of LATAM but extremely loyal once curated. Strong textile/craft culture (Mayan weavings) opens secondary product line (atelier weekends, craft sourcing trips for design buyers).

Lower revenue ceiling per chapter but excellent margins and brand-building value.

## Anchor regions

| Region | Vibe | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Antigua Guatemala** | Colonial UNESCO town | Volcanoes, cobblestone, top-tier boutique hotels (Casa Santo Domingo, Mesón Panza Verde) |
| **Lake Atitlán (Panajachel, San Marcos, Santa Cruz)** | Sacred volcanic lake | Three perfect-cone volcanoes around a 1,000-ft-deep lake. Each village has different vibe. |
| **Tikal (Petén)** | Pre-Maya pyramids in jungle | UNESCO ruins, jungle hike, howler monkeys |
| **Semuc Champey** | Turquoise limestone pools | Underrated, hard-to-reach, photographer's dream |
| **Acatenango Volcano** | Active volcano hike-and-camp | Watch Fuego erupt across the saddle |

## Risks

- **Security perceptions** — Guatemala has real risk in certain areas; stay in tourist corridor (Antigua, Atitlán, Tikal) and brief guests
- **Volcanic activity** — Fuego eruptions are scenic but Acatenango trips need safety buffer
- **Small market ceiling** — won't carry 4+ events/quarter at premium prices; keep cadence at 2-3
