# SORA · Chile Chapter

*Tier 2 · Target go-live: Q1 2028*

## Thesis

Chile has the highest GDP-per-capita in LATAM and the most stable institutions. That means premium guests, premium suppliers, premium prices. Four completely different products in one country: Santiago design + Valparaíso bohemian + Atacama desert + Patagonia. Most expensive setup of Tier 2 but highest revenue per event.

---

## Anchor regions

| Region | Vibe | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Santiago (Vitacura, Lastarria)** | Sophisticated capital | Restaurants, wine bars, design |
| **Valparaíso + Viña del Mar** | Bohemian port + beach | UNESCO hillside murals, Pablo Neruda house |
| **Atacama Desert (San Pedro)** | High-altitude moonscape | Tierra Atacama, Explora — top eco-luxury |
| **Patagonia (Torres del Paine)** | Wilderness icon | Explora Patagonia, EcoCamp — bucket-list trekking |
| **Wine country (Casablanca, Colchagua)** | Cool-climate wine | Within day-trip of Santiago |
| **Easter Island (Rapa Nui)** | Bucket-list outlier | Operationally complex, expensive |

## Real estate strategy

Chile real estate is the most "first-world" feeling in LATAM. Lower returns but stable.

- Santiago Vitacura/Las Condes apartments: $250k-$900k
- Coastal lots near Zapallar/Maitencillo: $200k-$1M
- Wine country plots Colchagua: $80k-$400k

**Brokers:** Engel & Völkers Chile · CB Richard Ellis · Mariana Aninat (Santiago boutique)

Commission: 2-3% (lowest in LATAM). Per closing: $5-25k.

## Risks

- **Chile is expensive** — every cost is higher than other LATAM. Pass-through pricing essential.
- **Patagonia season** Nov-March only
- **Easter Island access** — limited flights, mandatory advance booking
- **2019-style social unrest** — episodic; price-in some risk
