# SORA · Uruguay Chapter

*Tier 2 · Target go-live: Q1 2028 · Run as combined ops with Argentina*

## Thesis

Uruguay is the Hamptons of South America. Tiny country (~3.5M people), but Punta del Este and especially **José Ignacio** concentrate the wealthiest Argentines, Brazilians and Europeans during summer (December-March). Stable democracy, fully dollarized real estate, English-friendly. Off-season is empty and operationally not viable, so Sora-Uruguay is a **seasonal pop-up chapter** running December-March, shared ops with Argentina.

## Real estate strategy

Uruguay is small, premium, fully dollarized. Highest per-square-foot prices in LATAM.

- José Ignacio beachfront lots: $400k-$3M
- Punta del Este apartments: $250k-$1.5M
- Garzón countryside lots: $80k-$400k
- Tax-friendly (Uruguay welcomes foreign residents, "tax holiday" for new residents)

**Brokers:** Engel & Völkers Punta del Este · Inmobiliaria Cea (Punta) · Garzón Properties

Commission: 4-5%. Per closing: $10-50k.
