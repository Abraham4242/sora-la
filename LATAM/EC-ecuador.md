# SORA · Ecuador Chapter

*Tier 3 · Target go-live: Q1 2029*

## Thesis

Ecuador is the **eco-luxury** chapter. The Galápagos Islands alone make the country worth a chapter; layer in the Quito old town (UNESCO), Mindo cloud forest, Otavalo textiles, and Mompiche surf coast and you have a 5-day product menu that doesn't exist elsewhere. Dollarized economy (USD is the currency) → no FX friction. Small market but high willingness to pay for the Galápagos product specifically.

## Real estate strategy

Small market but dollarized = easy. Best plays:
- Quito Centro Histórico colonial: $200k-$800k
- Mindo cloud forest lots: $50k-$200k
- Cuenca colonial homes: $150k-$500k
- Mompiche beach lots: $50k-$250k

Galápagos real estate is **highly restricted** — only permanent residents can own. Skip.

**Brokers:** Sotheby's Ecuador · KW Ecuador (Quito) · Ecuador Properties (Cuenca)

Commission: 3-4%. Per closing: $5-25k.

## Risks

- **Galápagos park access** — strict daily-visitor caps; book 6 months out
- **Yacht-charter cancellation risk** — weather, mechanical; have backup land itinerary
- **Quito political demonstrations** — periodic; monitor
- **Mainland coast safety** (Guayaquil area) — avoid for events
