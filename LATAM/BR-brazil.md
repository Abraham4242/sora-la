# SORA · Brazil Chapter

*Tier 1 · Priority launch · Target go-live: Q3 2027*

## Thesis

Brazil is the aesthetic capital of Latin America. Bahia's beach towns (Trancoso, Caraíva, Praia do Forte), Fernando de Noronha, Búzios and Rio set the bar for what "beautiful weekend in LATAM" actually means. The operational complexity (Portuguese, complex tax, banking friction) keeps competitors out — which is exactly why Sora should be in. One Brazil weekend per quarter would sell from waitlist alone.

Run Brazil leaner than the others (fewer events, higher ticket prices, deeper margin) until volume justifies bigger ops.

## Real estate strategy

Brazil real estate is harder for foreigners than other LATAM markets but the upside is real, especially in Bahia and Floripa.

### Bahia · Trancoso / Caraíva
- Beach lots: $150k-$600k
- Existing boutique houses: $400k-$2M
- Trancoso has high turnover and Sora's profile of guest is exactly the buyer.

### Florianópolis · Jurerê / Lagoa
- Modern condos in Jurerê Internacional: $250k-$800k
- Mountain plots: $80k-$200k
- Growing tech-startup wealth driving prices.

### Rio · Ipanema / Leblon / Santa Teresa
- 2BR Ipanema apartment: $300k-$800k
- Santa Teresa restored casa: $250k-$700k
- USD-pricing has compressed since real depreciated.

**Recommended broker partners:**
- **Engel & Völkers Brazil** (multi-city, English-speaking)

Commission: typically 5-6% gross to selling broker, with the international brokerage taking a 20-30% override. Sora's net per closing: $8-50k.

## Legal entity setup

**Vehicle:** Sociedade Limitada (Ltda) — most flexible for foreign-owned operating companies.
- 1-2 quotaholders, no minimum capital
- Cost: $4-7k legal fees, ~60-90 days to form (Brazil is slow)

**Recommended firms:**
- **Mattos Filho** (top-tier, full-service, English-speaking partners)
- **TozziniFreire** (mid-tier, faster, good for SME setup)
- **Dechert / Stocche Forbes** (boutique with international experience)

**Critical:** every foreign shareholder needs a CPF (Brazilian tax ID) before the entity can be registered. Apply for CPF first — can be done remotely via the Brazilian consulate in your home country.

**Bank:** Banco Itaú or Bradesco for corporate. Brazil banking is bureaucratic; expect 30-60 days to fully open. USDC ramp via Mercado Bitcoin or Binance Brazil (regulated).

## Specific risks

- **Currency volatility** — BRL swings dramatically. Hedge by holding receipts in USD/USDC, only converting what you need for ops.
- **Tax complexity** — Brazil has the most complex tax code in LATAM. Mandatory accountant from day 1. Budget $800/mo for accounting alone.
- **Visa for Americans** — Brazil reinstated visa requirements for US tourists in April 2025. Make sure your guests know. E-visa is the new normal.
- **Carnival overcommit** — every operator over-promises during Carnival and quality drops. Lock in suppliers in October for February events.
- **Fernando de Noronha access** — daily visitor limits + park fees. Book 6 months out. Don't oversell.
