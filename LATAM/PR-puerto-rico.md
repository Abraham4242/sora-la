# SORA · Puerto Rico Chapter

*Tier 3 · Target go-live: Q4 2028 · Operates as US-domestic entity*

## Thesis

Puerto Rico is the easiest Caribbean chapter to set up operationally because it's a US territory — no passport needed for Americans, USD is the currency, US federal courts and laws apply, banking is US-domestic. **Act 60 tax incentives** (formerly Acts 20/22) have created a wealthy crypto + investor expat community in Dorado, Old San Juan, and Condado. That community is exactly the Sora customer: high-income, location-fluent, crypto-native, design-curious.

Smaller chapter (Puerto Rico is small geographically) but high-margin and easy operationally.

## Legal entity + ops

**Vehicle:** Puerto Rico LLC (registered under PR Department of State). Cost: $500 + $200/year filing. **~5 days.** Fastest of any chapter.

**Firms:** McConnell Valdés · Goldman Antonetti & Córdova · Pietrantoni Méndez & Álvarez

**Bank:** Banco Popular de Puerto Rico, FirstBank PR, or US banks operate (Chase, BofA). USDC: standard US-mainland processes.

**Hosts:** 1 in San Juan covering everywhere. $2,500-3,000/mo (PR salaries closer to US than LATAM).

**Talent:** José Enrique (chef at Cocina Abierta), Mario Pagán (Pagán restaurant), Antonio Bachour (pastry at Dorado Beach), local Vieques bio-bay naturalists.

## Risks

- **Hurricane season** Jun-Nov (especially Aug-Oct) — skip events these months
- **Puerto Rico political tension** around Act 60 + statehood debates — be culturally aware; don't lean into "tax haven" branding
- **Local-vs-expat tension** — PR has real resentment toward Act 60 buyers displacing locals. Be a thoughtful operator (hire local, source local, give back).

---

## Strategic role

PR is the **bridge chapter** between US mainland and the rest of Sora-LATAM. The Act-60 crowd lives in PR but books trips across all our chapters. Onboard them aggressively — they're the highest-LTV guest type in the network.

Also: PR is **a great content production base** — easy logistics, beautiful light, English-fluent crew.
