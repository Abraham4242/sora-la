# SORA · Peru Chapter

*Tier 2 · Target go-live: Q4 2027*

## Thesis

Lima is South America's food capital — top restaurants in the world's-50-best year after year (Central, Maido, Kjolle, Mayta). Plus the Sacred Valley and Machu Picchu remain the most powerful single bucket-list pull in LATAM. The Sora-Peru chapter is **gastronomy-first**, with Andean spirituality and beach (Máncora) as the secondary products.

---

## Anchor regions

| Region | Vibe | Why |

| **Sacred Valley (Pisac, Urubamba, Ollantaytambo)** | Andean culture | Higher-elevation lodges, archaeology, weaving |

## Risks

- **Altitude** at Cusco/Sacred Valley — brief guests, plan acclimatization day
- **Political instability** — recurrent protests, especially around Cusco. Monitor State Dept advisory.
- **Machu Picchu access** — daily visitor caps, must book 4 months ahead
- **Lima restaurant booking** — Central is booked 12 months out; build relationships now
