# SORA · Colombia Chapter

*Tier 1 · Priority launch · Target go-live: Q4 2026*

## Thesis

Colombia is the fastest-growing premium travel market in LATAM and the easiest of the Tier 1 chapters to set up operationally (SAS in 2 weeks, no minimum capital, friendly to foreign founders). Medellín is the second-largest US expat city in LATAM after Mexico City. Cartagena is the most photographically saleable city in the region — a single sunrise on the walled city sells weekends. Crypto-native: Binance P2P, Daviplata, Bancolombia all play nice with USDC.

This chapter has the lowest setup risk in Tier 1 and should be opened immediately after Mexico.

## Real estate strategy

### Medellín · El Poblado / Laureles / El Tesoro

- 2BR penthouse in Laureles: $200k-$400k

## Legal entity setup

**Vehicle:** Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (SAS).
- 1 shareholder minimum, no minimum capital, fastest in LATAM to form (~2 weeks).
- Cost: $1,500-2,500 legal fees.

**Recommended firms:**
- **Brigard Urrutia** (top-tier, full service, more expensive)
- **Posse Herrera Ruiz** (mid-tier, faster, good for SAS setup)
- **Lloreda Camacho** (boutique, real estate strength)
- **Procolombia** (the government investment-promotion agency) — free advisory on entity setup for foreign investors

**Bank:** Bancolombia for corporate. USDC ramp via Binance P2P (huge volume), Daviplata for retail.

## Specific risks

- **Reputation overhang** for US travelers (cocaine/security stigma). The market that knows better doesn't care; the rest is filter for the wrong guest.
- **Cartagena Caribbean season** — June-October is wet, low-season for our weekends. Plan Cartagena events Nov-May.
- **Medellín dengue + Zika** in rainy season — brief guests.
- **Real estate over-foreign-ownership** in El Poblado pushing local prices up — be a thoughtful operator, partner with local-owned services.
