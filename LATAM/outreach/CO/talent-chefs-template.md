## Decision math (for Tier 1 chef partnership)

- 14 guests × $1,580 ticket (Cartagena weekend)
- Chef cost: $3,920 (14 × $280 Tier 1)
- F&B raw cost: $1,400 (14 × $100)
- Hotel: ~$6,500 (already booked)
- **Total weekend cost:** ~$13-15k
- **Gross:** $22,120
- **Margin:** **$7-9k** (~35%)
- Brand value of "Sora had Catalina Vélez" or "Sora had Carmen" is permanent
