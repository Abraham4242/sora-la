## What Colombia has that Mexico doesn't

- **Faster legal setup** (2 weeks vs ~30 days)
- **Lower minimum salary** for hosts (~$1.8k vs $2.2k)
- **Better USD/local arbitrage** for premium guests (COP weakened post-Petro)
- **Looser real estate restrictions** (no fideicomiso needed anywhere)
- **Stronger crypto rails** (Binance P2P is huge, Bancolombia/Daviplata integrate cleanly)

## What's different

- The **inaugural event is Cartagena**, not San Miguel — Walled City Nov 20-23 weekend. Lock in Casa San Agustín immediately.
- **No Day of the Dead** equivalent — instead use Cartagena's pre-Christmas season (high demand)
- Hosts in Cartagena need **boat captain** contacts (Rosario Islands trips). Different network than Medellín.
