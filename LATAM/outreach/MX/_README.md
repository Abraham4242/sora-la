## What to expect

- **Lawyers:** respond within 3-5 business days. Expect a 30-min intake call. Quote in 1 week.
- **Hosts:** for public postings, expect 15-40 replies within a week. Phone-screen the top 5.
- **Lodging:** group sales departments respond in 2-5 business days. Expect a rate sheet + availability.
- **Chefs:** harder to reach directly; better to go through their PR or restaurant manager. Some won't respond.

## After receiving responses

Track everything in a simple Notion or Sheet:
- Firm/person · Date sent · Date replied · Their response · Next step · Decision

- 1 broker contract signed
