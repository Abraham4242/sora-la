## Notes per chef tier

### Tier 1

- Expect a NO or no-reply 70% of the time. Don't take it personally.
- Often handled through PR firms. Identify the firm (look at chef's recent press) and approach them.
- Budget: $250-$500/pax for the chef portion alone (not including F&B costs)
- Book 6+ months out

### Tier 2

- Much higher response rate. Pujol-tier chefs say no; Casa Polanco GMs say yes.
- Budget: $120-$220/pax for chef portion
- Some willing to do "guest chef" at our Habitas Tulum / Hotel Matilda events — they cook in the host hotel's kitchen for one night

### After response

If they say yes:
- Get a written commitment (email confirmation is fine for first event)
- Pay deposit (typically 30-50%)
- Coordinate with the lodging partner if dinner is at their kitchen
- Include chef in Sora app event page + Instagram pre-announcement

If they decline:
- Ask for a referral to "someone you'd recommend for this kind of curated dinner"
- Often a sous chef or chef de cuisine from their orbit will say yes
- Don't burn the bridge — try again next quarter for a different event

## Decision math (for Pujol-level chef)

- 12 guests × $1,180 ticket (CDMX Roma Norte weekend)
- Chef cost: $4,800 (12 × $400)
- F&B raw cost: $1,800 (12 × $150)
- Hotel: ~$5,000 (already booked)
- **Total weekend cost:** ~$13-15k
- **Gross:** $14,160
- **Margin:** low or break-even on first event
- **Brand value:** very high — "Sora had Enrique Olvera" is a permanent asset

For Tier 1 chef, accept low margin on FIRST event. The story is worth more than the margin.
