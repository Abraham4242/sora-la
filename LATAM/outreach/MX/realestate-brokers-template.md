# Outreach · Real Estate Brokers · Mexico

For partnering with local brokers in our 3 target real estate zones: **Tulum**, **San Miguel de Allende**, **Valle de Bravo / Valle de Guadalupe**.

We're not buying as Sora-MX corporate — we are bringing premium buyers (our event guests) to brokers in exchange for commission split. Standard structure: **3% commission to the listing-side broker, we receive a referral fee equal to 25-35% of that 3%**.

Net to Sora per closing: $5,000 - $30,000 depending on property value.

### Estructura de la asociación

- Nosotros traemos al comprador **calificado, internacional, en zona**.
- Ustedes manejan la transacción de extremo a extremo: listado, due diligence, cierre.
- Comisión split: **25-35% de la comisión total** (típicamente, 0.75-1.05% del precio de venta) para SORA como referral fee.
- No buscamos ser brokers nosotros — ustedes son los expertos locales.

### Lo que pueden esperar de nosotros

- **3-5 compradores activos por trimestre** en el chapter de México una vez que estemos en marcha

## What to expect

- Brokers will respond fast (within 2-3 days) — they like deal flow
- They will ask for exclusivity. **Decline exclusivity** — work with 2 brokers per zone for first 6 months to see who delivers.
- Standard MLS-style referral agreement: 25% of the 3% commission. Push for 30-35% if you have leverage.
- They will ask to see proof of your buyer pipeline. Show them: number of confirmed guests in upcoming Sora-MX events, demographic profile, average net worth (anonymous).

---

## After they respond

1. Sign a 1-page Referral Agreement (find a template — De la Vega & Conde can draft this for $500)
2. Get their inventory list — pick 3-5 properties per zone that fit the Sora buyer profile
3. Add those properties to the SORA app's Real Estate section under the MX chapter
4. Track referrals: which guest, which broker, which property, did they buy
5. After 6 months, drop the weaker brokers and consolidate volume with the top performers
