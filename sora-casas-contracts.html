LAND

Land Option

  • Parties: owner + Sora Real Estate Panamá S.A.
  • Term: 12-month exclusive option, renewable 6 months
  • Option fee: $2,500 at signing (applied if exercised)
  • Strike: owner cost + $5–10K spread, locked
  • Exercise: within 60 days of a ≥25% buyer deposit
  • Protection: recorded in the Public Registry
BUILD

Fixed-Price Build

  • Parties: buyer + Sora (Promotora)
  • Fixed turnkey price; model + finish tier + lot
  • Cooling-off: 7 days; then 25–30% deposit non-refundable
  • Milestones: 15/15/15 · 5–10% occ. · 15–20% closing
  • Escrow: attorney-held, joint sign-off release
  • Warranty: 1-yr structural + 3% hold-back; delay penalty
LOI

Letter of Intent

  • Non-binding intent to buy/build a Sora model on a lot
  • Indicative price + finish tier + market
  • Reservation: $5,000 refundable deposit holds the lot 30 days
  • Contingencies: title diligence + source-of-funds (AML)
  • Visa path: FNV/QIV attorney referral in parallel
  • Expires in 14 days unless converted
