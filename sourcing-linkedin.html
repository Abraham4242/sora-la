02 · CÓMO POSTEAR SIN APARECER EN TU FEED
02 · HOW TO POST WITHOUT SHOWING IN YOUR FEED

Paso a paso.

Step by step.

Si la posición se postea desde tu perfil personal con "Hiring", llega a tu red. Esta es la única forma silenciosa.

If you post from your personal profile with "Hiring", your network sees it. This is the only silent route.

01

Confirmar que Sora tiene Company Page

Confirm Sora has Company Page

Si no existe: linkedin.com/company/setup/new. Crear "Sora Travel Agency" o "La Compañía / Sora". Sube logo + headline + descripción en 5 min. Esta página es la que postea, no tu perfil.

If it doesn't exist: linkedin.com/company/setup/new. Create "Sora Travel Agency" or "La Compañía / Sora". Upload logo + headline + description in 5 min. This page posts, not your profile.

02

Hacerse admin de la página

Make yourself page admin

Asegurarte que tu perfil tiene rol de Super Admin en la página Sora. Si la creas tú, automático. Si no, pedirle al creador que te agregue: Page Admin Tools → Manage admins.

Make sure your profile has Super Admin role on the Sora page. If you created it, automatic. If not, ask the creator to add you: Page Admin Tools → Manage admins.

03

Publicar el job desde "Post a job"

Publish via "Post a job"

Ir a linkedin.com/talent/post-a-job. Verificar que el dropdown "Company" muestra Sora Travel Agency, no tu nombre. Si muestra tu nombre, cambiarlo. Este es el momento crítico.

Go to linkedin.com/talent/post-a-job. Confirm the "Company" dropdown shows Sora Travel Agency, not your name. If it shows your name, change it. This is the critical step.

04

NO marcar "Share with my network"

DO NOT check "Share with my network"

En la sección de promoción, LinkedIn te ofrece una casilla "Share this job on your profile" o "Notify your network". Dejarla SIN MARCAR. Si la marcas, broadcast inmediato a tus conexiones.

In the promotion section, LinkedIn offers a checkbox "Share this job on your profile" or "Notify your network". Leave it UNCHECKED. Checking it means instant broadcast to your connections.

05

Elegir tipo de posting

Choose posting type

Free Job (gratis, 1 activo por página): aparece pero LinkedIn no lo distribuye agresivamente. Promoted Job (pagado, CPC): aparece en perfil + se distribuye a candidatos que matchean. Recomendamos Promoted para Wave 1.

Free Job (free, 1 active per page): appears but LinkedIn doesn't push it aggressively. Promoted Job (paid, CPC): appears on profile + distributes to matching candidates. We recommend Promoted for Wave 1.

06

Configurar applicant routing a Calendly

Configure applicant routing to Calendly

En "How candidates apply" elegir External website y pegar el link de Calendly de Minor. Así los aplicantes saltan el CV de LinkedIn y agendan directamente. Reduce ruido 60%.

In "How candidates apply" select External website and paste Minor's Calendly link. This skips LinkedIn's CV form and they book directly. Cuts noise 60%.

07

Verificar en incógnito

Verify in incognito

Abrir LinkedIn en navegador incógnito sin tu sesión. Ir a tu perfil público. Si el job aparece bajo "Experiencia" o "Actividad reciente" → es público para tu red. Volver al paso 04 y desactivar share.

Open LinkedIn in incognito without your session. Go to your public profile. If the job appears under "Experience" or "Recent activity" → it's visible to your network. Go back to step 04 and disable share.

VERIFICACIÓN FINAL · OBLIGATORIA FINAL VERIFICATION · MANDATORY

Después de postear, pídele a alguien NO conectado a tu LinkedIn que busque tu nombre. Si ven el job posting bajo tu perfil, hay broadcast. Si no lo ven, está silencioso. Esta verificación se hace cada vez.

After posting, ask someone NOT connected to your LinkedIn to search your name. If they see the job under your profile, there's broadcast. If they don't, it's silent. Do this verification every time.

NUNCA HACER NEVER DO

Cero plantillas idénticas. LinkedIn detecta envíos masivos y reduce deliverability. Cambia mínimo el saludo + 1 frase específica por persona. Cero emojis en InMail profesional. Tasa de respuesta baja 8% con emojis en LATAM. Cero "espero que estés bien" — desperdicio de las primeras 8 palabras (lo que LinkedIn muestra en preview).

Zero identical templates. LinkedIn detects mass sends and reduces deliverability. Change at least the greeting + 1 specific sentence per person. Zero emojis in professional InMail. Reply rate drops 8% with emojis in LATAM. Zero "hope you're doing well" — wastes the first 8 words (what LinkedIn shows in preview).
