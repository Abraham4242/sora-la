NOTAS DE OPERACIÓN OPERATIONAL NOTES

Precios son solo del espacio (rental del venue + servicio mínimo). Comida/bebida se cotiza aparte por persona, típicamente $80–250 USD/pp en LATAM premium. Algunos venues requieren consumo mínimo en lugar de rental fee. Reservar 4-8 semanas antes para sábados en temporada alta.

Prices are space only (venue rental + minimum service). Food/beverage quoted separately per person, typically $80–250 USD/pp in premium LATAM. Some venues require minimum consumption instead of rental fee. Book 4-8 weeks ahead for high-season Saturdays.
