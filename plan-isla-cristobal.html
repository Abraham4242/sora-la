01 · THE LOCATION

Panamanian Caribbean. Archipelago without hurricanes.

Isla Cristóbal is the second-largest island in Bocas del Toro archipelago — between Isla Colón (the capital island) and Isla Bastimentos. Mangrove to the south, Caribbean beach to the north. No hurricanes in documented history. 18 min by boat from Bocas Town.

WHY ISLA CRISTÓBAL

Bocas without the Colón saturation

Isla Colón (Bocas Town) is saturated — hostels, parties, noise. Isla Cristóbal is right next door, 18 min by boat, and almost undeveloped for tourism. It's the 'next-up' play: the party circuit is already there, but the calm is still available.

  • Ngäbe-Buglé indigenous community present · respect + prior agreement needed
  • Sandy beachfront · no cliffs · friendly access
  • No hurricanes · Central American Caribbean spared historically
  • Bocas airport (BOC) · 60 flights/wk to PTY + COL · 1h
  • Friendly-nations visa + tourism permits already established in zone
  • Energy via solar + boat-fuel · water via rain catchment + osmosis

05 · PHASING

Four phases. Start with the discoteca.

The discoteca is the commercial anchor — opens first to drive traffic and proof the logistics (transport, energy, waste). Villas and hostal are phases 2-4.

01
2027 Q2-Q4
Agreement + permits + community agreement

Right-of-use agreement with Ngäbe-Buglé community (essential — without this, no project). ANATI + ARAP (maritime) + MINSA + ANAM (environmental EIA) permits. Panamanian company structure.

02
2028 Q1-Q4
Infra · discoteca · reception

Pilings and over-water platform. Solar + battery. Water system. Boats. Reception/restaurant building. Discoteca complete with soft opening October 2028 (end of rainy season).

03
2029 Q1-Q3
Hostal + 3 beachfront villas

Hostal 24 beds operational April 2029 (high dry season). V1, V2, V3 delivered in pairs. Dual operation: backpackers in hostal + couples in villas; both converge at discoteca.

04
2029 Q4 – 2030 Q3
V4 + V5 over-water + water-sports

V4 beachfront + V5 over-water (the 'flagship suite'). Water-sports center: kayak, SUP, snorkel, sunset cruise. Full capacity by high-season 2030-31.
